# The Work

Five services. Each one has a clear scope, a clear deliverable, and a clear way to measure whether it worked.

We price by the job, not by the hour, wherever we can. You should know what it'll cost before we start, and we shouldn't get paid more for taking longer.

> Pricing, durations, and team size below are starting assumptions. Adjust as real engagements show what the work actually takes.

---

## 1. The Walkthrough
*A diagnostic. We walk every room before we touch anything.*

**For:** Businesses that know something's off ("everything takes too long," "we're drowning in tools," "the numbers never match") but can't point to where.

**What we do**
- Interview people at every level, starting with the ones closest to the work
- Map how work actually flows (not how the org chart says it flows)
- Inventory the tools: what you pay for, what gets used, who owns it, what's held together with tape
- Pull the baseline numbers: cycle times, error rates, manual hours, spend

**What you get: a Condition Report**
- What's broken, roughly what it costs you, and the evidence
- What to fix first, what can wait, and what to leave alone
- The baseline we'd measure any future work against
- An honest read on whether you need us for the next step. Often you won't.

**Typical shape:** 2–3 weeks · fixed fee · the report is yours whether or not you hire us again

## 2. The Deep Clean
*An operational cleanup project, scoped from the Walkthrough.*

**For:** A specific, known mess: a broken handoff, a process that's grown six extra steps, a reporting cycle that eats a week every month.

**What we do**
- Redesign the process with the people who run it
- Fix, reconfigure, or remove the tools involved
- Document the new way so it holds after we leave
- Train the owners. There has to be an owner.

**How we measure:** Against the Walkthrough baseline. Before we start, we agree in writing on 2–4 metrics (e.g., "month-end close from 9 days to 5," "manual touchpoints per order from 7 to 3"). We report on all of them at the end, including the ones that didn't move.

**Typical shape:** 6–12 weeks · fixed scope, fixed fee · milestone billing

## 3. The Supply Closet
*Tools and technology rationalization.*

**For:** Companies with more software than they can explain. The seventeen-tool workflow. The integration that lives in one former employee's personal automation account.

**What we do**
- Full inventory: licenses, seats, usage, contracts, renewal dates, owners
- Map overlaps, gaps, and single points of failure
- Recommend what to keep, consolidate, replace, or cancel, with the switching cost of each
- Run the consolidation if you want us to, or hand you the plan if you don't

**How we measure:** Annual spend before/after, number of tools, number of undocumented integrations, and time-to-onboard a new hire onto the stack.

**Typical shape:** 3–6 weeks for the audit · consolidation scoped separately

## 4. The AI Reality Check
*An honest inspection of AI plans, pilots, and vendor promises.*

**For:** Leaders under pressure to "do something with AI." Teams whose pilot looked great in the demo and hasn't shipped. Anyone holding a vendor proposal with the word "autonomous" in it.

**What we do**
- Look at the specific use case, not AI in general
- Test it against *your* data, *your* edge cases, and *your* volume, not the vendor's demo set
- Check readiness: is the data clean enough, is the process stable enough, is there an owner for when it's wrong?
- Estimate real costs: licenses, integration, human review, and error handling

**What you get:** One of three verdicts per use case, with evidence:
- **Ready.** It works on your data today. Here's the rollout plan and what to watch.
- **Not yet.** Here's what has to be true first (usually cleanup work), and roughly what that costs.
- **Not this.** The tool can't reliably do the job. Here's what would work instead, which is sometimes a better spreadsheet.

**How we measure:** For anything we green-light: accuracy on your real cases, human review time, cost per transaction, and error rate, before and after.

**Typical shape:** 2–4 weeks per use case · fixed fee

> We're not anti-AI. We're anti-pretending. When it's ready, we'll say so, and we'll help you ship it.

## 5. The Night Shift
*Ongoing maintenance. A light retainer for keeping things clean.*

**For:** Past clients who want a second set of eyes, and teams without a dedicated ops or systems person.

**What we do**
- A monthly walkthrough of the metrics we set up together
- On-call for "is this vendor telling the truth?" and "should we buy this?"
- Small fixes before they become big messes
- A quarterly condition report

**Typical shape:** Monthly retainer · capped hours · cancel any month, no penalty

## What we don't do

- **We don't sell software** or take referral fees from vendors. Our recommendations aren't for sale.
- **We don't install a proprietary methodology.** No frameworks with trademarks, no certifications, no new vocabulary for your team to learn.
- **We don't create dependency.** If you still need us for the same thing a year later, we did it wrong.
- **We don't take work we can't measure.** If we can't agree on what "better" looks like, we're not ready to start.
- **We don't oversell.** If the honest answer is "this will help a little," we'll say that.
