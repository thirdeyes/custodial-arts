# Launch Checklist

A practical list of what to set up. This isn't legal, tax, or financial advice. Confirm the specifics with an attorney and an accountant for your state or country.

## Name & identity
- [ ] **Trademark search** for "Custodial Arts" in consulting/business services (in the US, the USPTO trademark search; elsewhere, your local registry). Consider filing once you're confident in the name.
- [ ] **Domain.** Check `custodialarts.com` and alternatives (`.co`, `.consulting`, `custodialarts.co`). Register a couple of close variants and redirect them.
- [ ] **LinkedIn company page** and matching personal headline
- [ ] **Business email** on the domain (e.g., `hello@` and your name)
- [ ] **Pop-culture caution:** The *Breakfast Club* janitor is a great origin story to *tell* in conversation and posts. Don't use film stills, clips, logos, or quoted dialogue in branded materials like the site, decks, or merch without the rights.

## Legal & financial
- [ ] Form the entity (LLC is common for solo consultancies)
- [ ] EIN / tax registration
- [ ] Business bank account and a separate card
- [ ] Bookkeeping setup
- [ ] **Professional liability (E&O) insurance.** Many clients require it before signing.
- [ ] General liability insurance if you'll be on client premises

## Paperwork templates
- [ ] **Master Services Agreement (MSA):** have a lawyer draft it once and reuse it
- [ ] **Statement of Work (SOW) template** covering scope, deliverables, **agreed metrics & baseline**, fee, and timeline. Putting the metrics in the SOW turns "measure before we mop" into a contract term.
- [ ] **Mutual NDA**
- [ ] **Condition Report template**, the Walkthrough deliverable
- [ ] **Case note template**, a one-page anonymized before/after

## Presence
- [ ] Deploy the site in `/site` (see README)
- [ ] Publish the launch post (see [linkedin.md](../content/linkedin.md))
- [ ] Send the forwardable paragraph (see [word-of-mouth.md](word-of-mouth.md)) to 20 people who know your work

## Pricing (decide before the first call)
- [ ] Walkthrough fixed fee
- [ ] Deep Clean pricing approach (fixed per scope)
- [ ] Night Shift monthly retainer and hour cap
- [ ] Your floor: the smallest engagement worth taking
