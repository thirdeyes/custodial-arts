# Voice & Tone

**In one line:** We sound like the smartest person in the building, the one who's never been asked to speak at the all-hands.

Dry, warm, and plainspoken. We dress essential things up in fancy words as a joke, and the joke makes a point: this work matters. We never dress up the *results*.

---

## The four dials

| Dial | Where we sit | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Formal ↔ Casual | Casual, with good posture | "Here's what we found." Not "Per our assessment…" |
| Serious ↔ Funny | Serious about the work, light about ourselves | The joke is about the mess, and the numbers are real. |
| Confident ↔ Humble | Confident about what we know, open about what we don't | "We don't know yet. We'll know by Thursday." |
| Salesy ↔ Matter-of-fact | Matter-of-fact, always | We describe. We don't pitch. |

---

## The joke, and its limits

**The joke:** We use gallery, museum, and fine-arts language for janitorial work, and janitorial language for consulting work.

- "The Permanent Collection" means the messes we see over and over
- "Condition report" means our assessment
- "The Walkthrough", "The Deep Clean", "The Supply Closet", "The Night Shift" are our services
- "Elbow grease" is our methodology. (It really is.)

**The limits:**
- **We punch at messes, never at people.** A broken process isn't anyone's fault until proven otherwise, and it usually isn't anyone's fault after that either. We never mock a client, a client's staff, or someone who built the thing we're replacing. They were doing their best with what they had.
- **We don't use humor to dodge.** If the news is bad, we say it plainly first. The joke, if there is one, comes after.
- **We don't mock janitors.** The whole premise is that custodial work is expert work. The humor is aimed at corporate pretension, not at the people doing the real job.
- **We don't overdo it.** One wink per post, email, or page is plenty. If every sentence is a mop pun, it stops being funny.

---

## Words

**We use:** mess, clean up, fix, untangle, baseline, measured, actually, honestly, here's what we found, we don't know yet, you probably don't need this, handoff, owner, in plain terms.

**We avoid:**

| Instead of… | Say… |
|---|---|
| Transform / transformation | Fix, improve, rebuild. And say by how much. |
| Leverage | Use |
| Synergy | *(delete the sentence)* |
| Seamless | It works, and here's where it still doesn't |
| Best-in-class / world-class | *(show the result instead)* |
| AI-powered / AI-first | Say what the tool actually does |
| 10x / revolutionary / game-changing | The real number |
| Unlock value | Save time / save money / reduce errors, with the number |
| Solutions | The fix |
| Thought leadership | Things we've learned the hard way |

---

## Numbers

- Always say where a number came from: "measured over 6 weeks," "client's own ticket data," "our estimate."
- Report ranges when we're uncertain. Report misses alongside wins.
- Never round up to sound impressive. 18% stays 18%, not "nearly 20%."
- An estimate is labeled as an estimate every time it appears.

## Examples

**Proposal opener**
> ❌ "Custodial Arts will leverage best-in-class methodologies to transform your operational landscape."
> ✅ "You told us month-end close takes nine days and nobody knows why. We'll spend two weeks finding out, then tell you what it'd take to get it to five."

**Status update with bad news**
> ❌ "We've encountered some exciting opportunities for optimization in the data layer!"
> ✅ "The CRM data is in worse shape than either of us expected. About 30% of accounts are duplicates. That adds two weeks. Here are three options."

**Declining work**
> ✅ "Honestly, you don't need a consultant for this. Your ops lead already knows the fix. Give them two weeks without meetings and they'll get it done. Call us if that doesn't work."

**On AI**
> ❌ "Unlock the power of AI to revolutionize your customer experience."
> ✅ "The support chatbot can handle password resets and order status. That's about 20% of your tickets. It can't handle the other 80% yet, and forcing it will cost you customers. Start with the 20%."
